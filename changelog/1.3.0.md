- A Reading list for what you mean to read. Select bookmarks and press R, or choose Send to Reading in a bookmark's menu or on the selection bar; Reading sits under Favorites and counts what is still unread, newest first.
- A bookmark remembers how far you read it and whether you finished it. Mark as read and Mark as unread are in its menu; read ones move to the bottom and leave Reading after a week.
- Sending to and taking out of Reading can be undone with Ctrl Z. Reading, progress and read state are kept in a full copy and sync between your computers.
- AI assistants can search the Reading list. They can't add to it.
- After this update, an earlier version of Koona Pin can't open the library, and computers syncing together need this version on each of them.
