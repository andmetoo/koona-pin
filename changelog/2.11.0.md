- On the phone, swipe left or right on the list to move between filters: All, Favorites and your collections in Library, your feeds in Feeds.
- Reading has two pages, To read and Read, with a switch at the top; swipe or tap between them.
- A finished article now moves to Read instead of leaving Reading. Reading to the end finishes it by itself, so the button at the article's end is gone.
