- Articles in Reading show their lead photo on top, as on the site. News sites like The Verge, TechCrunch, Wired and Ars Technica used to open with no picture at all.
- Pictures that load as you scroll on the site now show in Reading and in feed posts, as do pictures that sites only offer in newer formats.
- Pictures from sites still on plain http now load over https instead of being left out, in articles, feed posts and feed thumbnails.
- A picture that can't load is hidden instead of showing a broken icon. Small badges and icons stay in the line of text, and emoji drawn as pictures become real emoji.
