- Reading mode: press «Read» to read saved articles in one calm column, without menus or banners, one after another with J and K.
- A Reading list in the sidebar: send links there with R, from the card menu, or with «Save to Reading» in the browser extension (Alt+Shift+R). Read articles move down and leave after a week.
- In reading mode, Tab moves between the queue and the article, and ⌫ takes the current article out of Reading.
- A click now selects a bookmark. The title, the thumbnail and the shutter over a cover open the link, and Space reads it in a sheet over the list.
- The arrow keys move the selection in the grid and the list; with Shift they extend it.
- The Aa panel sets text size, column width, theme and font. White and Dark are free; Paper and Sepia come with Supporter.
- Settings → Reading: open links in the browser or in reading mode, and mark articles as read at the end.
- With Supporter, your Reading list, reading progress and read state sync between your computers.
- On a Mac, shortcuts show as ⌘, and ⌫ or ⌘⌫ moves the selected bookmarks to the Trash.
- A link to a page that redirects elsewhere is saved with the target page's title.
- Titles of pages in older encodings, such as windows-1251, are saved readable.
- In Safari, the menu says «Save page to Reading in Koona Pin», and a save no longer fails when Safari refuses to store the queue.
- The privacy policy now says that reading mode requests the page you open from its site, and the next article in the queue ahead of time.
