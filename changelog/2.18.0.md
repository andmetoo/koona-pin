- Click a picture in Reading or in a feed post to see it larger: it opens in a card over the article and grows out of its place in the text. Zoom with the wheel, a pinch, a double click or + and -, drag to look around, and close with Esc, the close button or a click beside it. On a phone, pinch to zoom and swipe down or press Back to close.
- A picture that links to a page offers that link in the card.
