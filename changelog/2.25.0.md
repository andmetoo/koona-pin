- The sidebar's main button, «New link» («Follow a site» in Feeds), is now a green key with a paperclip (a feed mark in Feeds) and its shortcut in a small key of its own. In the dark theme it stays a calm green instead of turning near-white.
- Switching between Bookmarks and Feeds no longer nudges the tab labels sideways.
- The Bookmarks and Feeds tabs now work like tabs from the keyboard: Tab reaches the chosen one, and the left and right arrow keys switch modes.
- Reading: the reading sheet now closes the way it opens, sliding down and fading out with the dimmed background, which now fades in too. It no longer shifts sideways while it opens or closes.
- Reading: a page that can't be read shows a small card with the reason instead of a full empty sheet.
- Reading mode: J and K no longer draw a focus outline around the whole article.
- Selecting a card in a short list no longer shifts the grid sideways; Settings, Feeds and the other scrolling panes keep still the same way.
- On a Mac, lists and columns use the system's scrollbars, hidden until you scroll, instead of bars that were always shown.
