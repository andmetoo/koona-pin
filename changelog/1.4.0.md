- A click on a bookmark now selects it, it no longer opens the page. Ctrl+click adds one more, Shift+click a range, and dragging a frame still selects many. The checkbox that appeared on hover is gone.
- To open a page: in the list, click its title or picture; in the grid, point at the picture and choose Open. Double-click and Enter open it too.
- Read a bookmark without leaving the library: select it and press Space, or choose Read on the selection bar or on the picture. The article opens over the list; ↑ and ↓ go through the other selected bookmarks, Esc closes it.
- The article shows where you stopped reading, and reading to the end marks it as read. When a page can't be shown, Open in browser is one click away.
