- AI assistants on your computer can search your library: Claude Desktop, Claude Code, the ChatGPT desktop app, Cursor and other apps that work with MCP. Turn it on in Settings → AI assistants; it is off until you do.
- Choose what an assistant may do: only read, or also add links to Inbox, tag them, move them to a collection and mark favorites. Each change can be undone with Ctrl Z, and an assistant can never delete anything.
- «How to connect…» shows the steps for each assistant with the command or settings to copy; Claude Desktop installs Koona Pin in one click.
- Links an assistant saves wait in Inbox, marked with its name: «saved from Claude Code».
- The «Connect browser» window no longer changes its height when you switch between browsers.
