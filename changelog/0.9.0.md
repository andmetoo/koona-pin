- Supporters can keep their automatic backups in a folder of their choice, such as one that Dropbox or Google Drive keep in the cloud: open Supporter in the sidebar and choose the folder. Every automatic backup is copied there too, and old ones are pruned the same way as on this computer; nothing else in that folder is touched.
- If the folder can't be reached, the Supporter dialog says so, and the backups on this computer go on as before.
