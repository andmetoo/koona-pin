- On a Mac the window has no grey title bar of its own: the traffic lights sit on the app, in line with the toolbar, and the window drags by the toolbar and the top of the sidebar.
- The sidebar keeps the logo, «New link» and the sections in place; feeds, collections and tags scroll between them and slip under a frosted footer, without a scrollbar flashing as reading mode opens.
- The toolbar and the reading bar are frosted glass: cards and text scroll under them and show through softly, as in Safari.
- A title, a double-click and Enter now open a bookmark in reading mode; «In browser» is still one click away in Settings → Reading.
- A new library starts in the compact grid.
- Dialogs open the way «New link» does: frosted glass over a softly blurred window.
- A click on the logo takes you to All bookmarks.
- Buttons, headings, cards and settings no longer get selected like text on a web page when you click and drag; the article you read, notes and fields still select as before.
