- Reading mode has an Aa button: make the text larger or smaller, choose a narrow, medium or wide column, a serif or sans font, and a White or Dark page. By default the page follows the app's theme, and your choice stays on this computer.
- Supporters also get the warm Paper and Sepia themes and two more fonts, Book and Legible.
- Articles now read in Literata, the serif the reading mode was designed with.
- Settings has a new Reading section. Choose whether a click on a title, a double-click and Enter open links in your browser or in reading mode, and whether an article you read to the end is marked as read.
