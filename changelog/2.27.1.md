- Lists: a `.kpin` list holding a number at the very edge of what the format allows, such as the largest number a computer's double can hold written out in full, now opens. Before, Koona Pin refused it as not a list, though the number was in range.
- Lists: the `.kpin` specification now says what Koona Pin already did: a list with a number too large to hold, such as `1e400`, or with arrays and objects nested more than 127 levels deep, is not a list.
- Reader: an article you scroll to the end of while it is still opening now counts as read that far, without scrolling again.
