- Feeds: follow a site's RSS, Atom or JSON feed and read its new posts in Koona Pin, in three columns — your feeds in the sidebar, the posts, and the article in a calm sheet set like reading mode. Feeds are free.
- Paste a link into «New link»: you see the page's real title and picture before you save, and when the site has a feed, a «Follow» switch (off until you turn it on) and where new posts go — only to Feeds, into the Inbox to sort like other new links, or with a notification too.
- A post becomes a bookmark only when you keep it: S saves it to the library, R to Reading, F to Favorites, O opens it in the browser; J and K move through the list, U marks it read or unread. Unsaved posts are kept for 30 days.
- The app fetches feeds itself, straight from the sites, about once an hour or as often as you choose in Settings → Feeds; nothing goes through a server. With no network, the posts already fetched stay readable.
- Import and export your feeds as OPML, from «More» or Settings → Feeds.
- With Supporter sync, the feeds you follow travel between your computers; posts and read marks stay on each one. Update every computer to 2.0: sync pauses between 1.x and 2.0 until both are updated.
- Drag selected bookmarks onto Favorites or Reading in the sidebar to star them or send them to Reading, as you already could onto a collection, a tag or Trash.
