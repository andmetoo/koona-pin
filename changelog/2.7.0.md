- Koona Pin on Android gets its own phone interface, ready for its first testers: Inbox sorting with swipes and Undo, the library as a list or a grid, search, the reader, feeds, a New link sheet, and You & sync.
- On a Galaxy Z Fold and other unfolded foldables, a navigation rail on the side you hold and the open link or post beside the list; folding and unfolding keeps your place and what you were typing.
- On a phone, type a link into New link and press Go to see the page and whether the site has a feed before you save it; press Go again to save.
- New link finds a site's feed more often: when the page you give names no feed, or a feed's address doesn't open (`/rss` where the site keeps `/rss.xml`), it looks on the site's home page.
- The phone follows the system: dark mode, text size up to 200 %, touch feedback, and battery saver, which turns the glass effects off.
- The phone app is translated into every language of the desktop app.
- On the computer, only New link changes: it finds a site's feed more often, as above.
