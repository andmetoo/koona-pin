- Save to Reading from the browser: right-click a page or a link and choose Save to Reading, or press Alt+Shift+R (Option+Shift+R on a Mac). It needs the browser extension 0.11.0.
- A new page saved this way waits in the Inbox as before and is in Reading too. A page you saved earlier is just added to Reading, with your tags and notes as they were.
- Saved while Koona Pin was closed, it goes to Reading as soon as the app starts.
