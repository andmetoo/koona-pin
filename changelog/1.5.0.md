- Reading mode: press Read in the top right corner to read your Reading list one article after another. The queue stays on the left, the article sits in a calm column in the middle, and a line at the top shows how far you've read.
- J and K go to the next and previous article, O opens the current one in your browser, and Esc takes you to the queue and then back to the library, right where you left it.
- An article you read to the end is marked as read and moves aside in the queue. The title of the queue switches it to the section you were looking at instead of Reading.
- When a page can't be shown, the article column offers Open in browser, Next and Mark as read. Without a connection, Koona Pin tries again by itself once you're back online.
- In a narrow window the queue waits behind the Queue button.
