- Sorting the Inbox is calmer: the bookmark sits on a stack of the next ones, and one capsule decides with «← Move to trash | Keep →» and the heart. A short note at the bottom says what Z will undo.
- Collections show as rounded chips with their number, and the suggested one is edged in its own colour. Collection colours are clearer in both themes.
- When the Inbox is sorted, three tiles count what you kept, filed and moved to the trash.
- A bell in the sidebar collects new bookmarks waiting in the Inbox, the offer to import from your browser and problems with saving from the browser. The library no longer shows banners for them.
- «New link» is at the top of the sidebar (⌘N or Ctrl N). It shows a preview, saves straight to the library, the Inbox or Reading, and offers the collection where links from that site usually go.
- Paste a link anywhere in the library with ⌘V or Ctrl V to save it at once, or drag a link onto the window; hold Shift to send it to the Inbox.
- Collections and Tags in the sidebar fold away with a click on their heading.
