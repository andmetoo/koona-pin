- Cards look calmer: Open, Read and ⋯ are small glass buttons in the top-right corner of a card, with their names in hints.
- A bookmark without a picture shows the site's own name in large type across its cover, such as «Serious Eats» or «The Verge». The name comes from the page; until a bookmark is saved again or «Refresh preview» reads it, the domain stands in.
- A selected card sinks into a frosted frame made of its own picture, with a check in the corner.
- A compact grid beside Grid and List: smaller cards, more of them in a row.
- Search moves to the sidebar, under the logo; in a narrow window it stays in the toolbar.
- The sidebar, toolbar and menus are more precise, the ⋯ menu is frosted glass, and the dark theme is deeper.
- Saved articles can keep an offline copy of their text and pictures on your computer, to read without a connection. Copies are never uploaded or synced and can be removed in Settings.
- Sync: computers on 1.8.0 pause sync with computers on an older version until those are updated too.
