- On Android, Koona Pin is in the Share menu of any app: a shared link lands in your Inbox, even when Koona Pin is closed. A small sheet says «Saved to Koona Pin» and you stay where you were.
- The same link shared twice is still one bookmark.
- In Inbox on the computer, a link shared from a phone app says it came from another app.
