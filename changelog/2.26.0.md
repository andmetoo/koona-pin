- The library: the grid ends where the toolbar above it does, measured against the scrollbar each window really has: the thin bars of Windows and Linux, a Mac's bars that show only while you scroll, or the bars a Mac keeps with a mouse. Duplicates, Link check, Settings, Sort inbox, the bookmark editor and the Feeds list stand as far in on the right as on the left, and the text of reading mode, the reading sheet and a feed post stays centred.
- List view: each row's picture and the group names start under the title, as the cards do in the grid.
- Sort inbox in a narrow window stands 24 px in, as every other page does.
- Settings: the language list is as tall as the buttons beside it.
- Every page title now stands in the same place: switching between Bookmarks and Feeds, or opening Duplicates, Link check, Settings or Sort inbox, no longer moves the title up, down or sideways, and «All feeds» has the same type as «Reading».
- Sort inbox: the progress dots narrow when the row runs short, so a long title in another language keeps its words and Finish stays beside it.
- Feeds: the posts, the day labels and the search field now start under «All feeds», as the cards do under «Bookmarks»; the toolbar over a post stands as far in on the left as on the right.
- Reading mode and the reading sheet: all their buttons have the same corners.
- Sidebar: the icon and label of «New link» and «Follow a site» stand in line with the rows under them.
- Feeds on a Mac: the cards end where the header's buttons do, and stand as far in on both sides.
- The picture viewer: a picture opens at least as large as it stood in the text. A feed's lead picture, often a small file stretched across the post, opened smaller than it was shown.
- Reading mode: leaving it puts the focus back on «Read» again, with newer versions of the system's web view on Windows too.
- The library runs on SQLite 3.54 (from 3.46), and the app's other components are updated to their current versions.
