- The search is one field in the toolbar in every window, so the sidebar has room for «New link» and your collections.
- In the Inbox, a small card shows the newest bookmarks, how many wait and where they came from, with «Sort»; it stays until the Inbox is empty.
- The notification panel always opens above the library and the sidebar, scrollbars included.
- A notification no longer says «2,559 days ago» for bookmarks imported from a browser: times show only for the last week.
- Settings → Data can refresh the previews of all bookmarks, or only those without a picture or site name, and selected bookmarks can be refreshed from the selection bar; imported bookmarks get their pictures, icons and site names in the background right after an import, and pages that don't open go to the link check, whose result shows in the notifications.
