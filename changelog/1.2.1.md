- Koona Pin for Mac is now signed by its developer and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app: no more Open Anyway in System Settings or commands in Terminal.
- Safari is the fourth browser: its extension comes with the Mac app, with nothing else to install. Turn it on in Safari → Settings → Extensions and allow it on websites; the button saves the page you are on, as in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. On a Mac, Connect browser now opens on Safari with these steps.
- The Mac disk image opens to a window that shows where to drag Koona Pin.
