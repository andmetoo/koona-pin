- Supporter can be bought in the Android app through Google Play: yearly, monthly or five years at once, at Google Play's prices. After paying, you give your email and the code sent to it, and Supporter is yours on every device.
- A Supporter plan paid through Google Play shows «Manage in Google Play», or «Fix payment in Google Play» when a renewal failed, on the phone and on the computer.
- On phones, Supporter, the sign-in and your plan open in place of «You & sync» instead of on top of it.
