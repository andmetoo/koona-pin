- A file dragged onto the window could replace the app with its raw text on a Mac. The app now takes the links, feed files and lists it can import, and ignores any other file you drop.
- Koona Pin lists (.kpin) have their own icon in the Finder on a Mac, in every view, instead of a preview of their text, and in File Explorer on Windows instead of the app's icon.
- A file dragged in after a card's picture was dragged is taken again: the list's pictures no longer start a drag of their own.
- A link or text dragged onto the search field goes into it.
- On iPhone, Supporter can be bought in the app through the App Store: monthly, yearly or once, with Restore purchases. A purchase made on your computer still counts on the phone.
- On iPhone, Koona Pin is in the share sheet of Safari and other apps: a shared link lands in the Inbox, even when the app is closed.
- On iPhone, the account and the sync keys are kept in the Keychain and stay on this phone, out of backups.
- Phones fetch site icons from the site itself instead of a third-party icon service.
- A new iPhone joins sync as "iPhone" and a letter instead of the name "Computer".
- On iOS 18 the screen no longer leaves an empty strip at the bottom.
- On phones, a purchase that is waiting to be confirmed can't be bought a second time by accident.
