- Bookmarks and Feeds are now two modes, each with its own sidebar. Switch with the tabs at the top of the sidebar, or with ⌘1 and ⌘2 (Ctrl 1 and Ctrl 2 on Windows and Linux). Koona Pin opens in the mode you used last, and each mode comes back where you left it.
- In Feeds, the main button is «Follow a site», and ⌘N follows a site too. A dot on the Feeds tab tells you there are new posts while you are in Bookmarks.
- Feeds has two new places: Today, with the day's posts from every feed, and «Saved from feeds», with every post you saved, even after the feed drops it.
- Saving a post now offers «Show it», which opens the new bookmark in Bookmarks.
