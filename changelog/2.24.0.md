- Bookmarks and Feeds are now two modes, each with its own sidebar. Switch with the tabs at the top of the sidebar, or with ⌘1 and ⌘2 (Ctrl 1 and Ctrl 2 on Windows and Linux). Koona Pin opens in the mode you used last, and each mode comes back where you left it.
- In Feeds, the main button is «Follow a site». It opens a sheet of its own: paste a site's address and Koona Pin follows its feed, without saving the page as a bookmark, and a page without a feed says so. ⌘N opens it in Feeds, and a dot on the Feeds tab tells you there are new posts while you are in Bookmarks.
- Feeds has two new places: Today, with the day's posts from every feed, and «Saved from feeds», with every post you saved, even after the feed drops it.
- Saving a post now offers «Show it», which opens the new bookmark in Bookmarks.
- «Add tag…» in a bookmark's menu now works: it opens the bookmark with the cursor in its tags.
- Smaller fixes: without the browser extension, the sidebar's row says «Connect browser»; tags no longer look cut off at the bottom of the sidebar; a clicked sidebar row no longer keeps a heavy outline; the notifications panel is easier to read; and the toolbar's «Read» is now «Reading mode».
