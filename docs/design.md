# Design basics

What a contributor needs to change the interface without breaking its look. The values below are the ones in code; `src/styles/app.css` is the single place for colours.

## Brand

- The accent colour is "envy green" `#2f6b4f` (light theme) and mint `#8fd0ae` (dark theme).
- The mascot, a small figure holding a paper clip, lives in `design/brand/pin-spirit-flat/`: `pin-spirit.svg` (green body, for light backgrounds), `pin-spirit-mint.svg` (dark backgrounds), `pin-spirit-cream.svg` (on green). Use it at 36 px and up.
- The icon is the one-colour mark `pin-spirit-micro.svg`, cream (`#faf7f0`) on an envy-green (`#2f6b4f`) rounded tile, at every size: the app on Windows, Linux and macOS, its tray. The site's favicon and the extension keep their first icons: the site has the mark (in its own repository, `andmetoo/koona-site`), and the extension keeps no tile in the browser's toolbar: the mark drawn like the mascot at 16–32 px, the mascot at 48 px and up. The macOS menu bar has the mark alone, as a template image the system paints.
- App and extension icons are generated by `bun scripts/brand-icons.mjs`; the macOS ICNS follows Apple's grid (an 824 px tile on 1024), so the Dock shows it without a plate of its own.
- The name, mascot and logo are trademarks; see [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md) before using them in a fork.

## Colour tokens

Tokens are CSS custom properties `--np-*`: light values on `:root`, dark values on `[data-theme="dark"]`. Tailwind 4 sees them through `@theme inline` as `--color-*`, so utilities like `bg-panel`, `text-subtle`, `border-border` and `bg-accent-soft` switch with the theme by themselves. Do not hard-code colours in components; add a token instead.

Also in the file: `--np-divider`, `--np-accent-muted`, `--np-suggested`, `--np-on-danger-soft`, `--np-scrim` (overlay behind dialogs and the editor in a narrow window), the five collection colours (`--np-terracotta`, `--np-green`, `--np-blue`, `--np-ochre`, `--np-lilac`), the update row in collection blue (`--np-update*`), a warm amber for update problems (`--np-amber*`), the menu's glass (`--np-menu-glass`, `--np-menu-shadow`), the field shadow `--np-shadow-1` and the dark scrollbar thumb `--np-thumb`. The `--np-read-*` tokens belong to the reading themes the user picks and do not follow the app's dark theme.

The dark theme is "deep": a near-black ground with surfaces lifted from it in steps of light (panel below the page, card and raised above it). Edges, lines, hovers and wells are white at a few per cent, so one value reads on the panel, the page and the card alike. Whatever is laid over another colour as a solid (secondary surfaces and their hover, toasts, soft tints, collection colours) stays opaque.

Shapes of the shell: sidebar rows 32 px with radius 9, the active one `bg-raised` with `shadow-raised` (a tiny shadow and the 1 px raised edge) and the accent icon; section headers tracked at 0.08em; tags as outlined chips (a 1 px line inside, filled on hover). Toolbar buttons have radius 9, a `line` border and `shadow-button`; the primary one adds `shadow-primary`, a light inset top edge. The grid/list switch is a segmented control: a `well` trough with the chosen segment `bg-raised shadow-segment`. Menus are glass (`menu-glass`): radius 13, the card at about 82 % over `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(170%)`, an inner hairline and a deep soft shadow, opaque where the blur is not drawn; items 30 px with radius 8.

The search sits in the sidebar under the logo (`search`: 36 px, radius 10, the card with a 1 px line and `--np-shadow-1`); in a narrow window, where the sidebar is a strip, it is in the toolbar as a soft well (`search` with `data-well`). Focused, both take the card, an edge of accent mixed 55 % into the border and a 4 px halo of accent at 20 %. The shortcut sits in a chip of its own on the right and steps aside while the empty field is focused; with a query it reads Esc.

Rules of thumb:

- Red is for errors only. Warnings and "needs attention" states use ochre or amber.
- The browser status uses collection green when connected, ochre when lost or outdated, `--np-danger` when failing.
- On the dark card, `--np-panel` reads as a hole; use `--np-secondary` for secondary surfaces there.

## Themes

The theme is the `data-theme` attribute (`light` or `dark`) on `<html>`, set before the first frame in `src/main.tsx` from the stored choice or `prefers-color-scheme`; "System" follows the media query live. Tailwind's `dark:` variant is defined with `@custom-variant` on `data-theme`; it is needed only where something other than a colour differs. Check every UI change in both themes.

## Typography

Inter (variable, from `@fontsource-variable/inter`, SIL OFL 1.1) is bundled, so no network is needed. Reading (NP-124) bundles Literata (variable, from Fontsource, SIL OFL 1.1), the serif of the Aa panel; its sans is Inter. Chinese, Japanese and Korean fall back to the system font of the interface language, chosen with `:lang()` on `<html>`; no CJK font is bundled.

Text sizes replace Tailwind's scale:

| Utility | Size | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `text-2xs` | 11 px | Section captions (caps, SemiBold), tag chips, badge digits |
| `text-xs` | 12 px | Domain and metadata, list group headings |
| `text-sm` | 13 px | Body text, buttons, menus (the default) |
| `text-md` | 14 px | Card and list row titles (Medium) |
| `text-lg` | 16 px | Wordmark |
| `text-xl` | 20 px | Bookmark title in triage |
| `text-2xl` | 22 px | Section heading (SemiBold) |

Radii: `rounded-sm` 6, `rounded-md` 7, `rounded-lg` 8 px for controls; `rounded-xl` 10 px for previews and banners; `rounded-2xl` 12 px for the action bar; `rounded-3xl` 14 px for the triage card. Spacing steps: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 24, 28, 32 px.

## Icons

- One set: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) through `lucide-react`, wrapped by `Icon` in `src/components/icons.tsx` at 16 px with a 1.5 px stroke.
- Drawing inside 14×14 of the 16×16 frame, round caps and joins, no fills except for an "on" state (a filled heart for favorites).
- Colour `--np-icon`, `--np-accent` when active.
- Own icons (`ClipIcon`, `InboxTray`) are made with `createLucideIcon` on the same grid. Do not paste icons from other sets as they are; redraw them to these rules.
- The logo in the sidebar is the `Mascot` component.

## Motion and stability

- Hover changes colour and border in 120–160 ms. Cards and images never move or scale.
- Panels and dialogs appear in about 200 ms: opacity and up to 5 px of movement (`animate-appear`).
- Animate named properties, never `transition: all`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` durations drop to zero globally.
- Space for previews, titles and metadata is reserved; late metadata never shifts the layout.
- Focus is always visible: a 2 px accent ring with a 2 px gap (`:focus-visible`); fields use the `field` utility (1 px border, accent border and 3 px halo on focus). Escape closes a layer and returns focus.

## Where things live

- Tokens, theme, fonts, base styles, utilities: `src/styles/app.css`.
- Shared components: `src/components/ui` (buttons, fields, switch, segmented control, menu, dialog, notices, key hints, window bar); shell: `src/components/shell`.
- Strings: only in `src/locales` ([translating.md](translating.md)).
- Minimum window size: 800×550.

Changing the look of a screen also changes the visual baselines; update them only after looking at the difference ([testing.md](testing.md#visual-baselines)).
