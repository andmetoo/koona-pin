- A new way to become a Supporter: five years paid once, for half the price of five yearly payments. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel; the Supporter dialog shows until when it is paid.
- Supporters with a subscription can switch to five years from the Supporter dialog: the five years start when the period you paid for ends, and the subscription stops renewing then, so you lose no day and never pay twice.
